RDS: testing whether additional ratio movements share the Issue 5 mechanism

Run each .sql file separately in Teradata SQL Assistant. No SAS, new engine run,
permanent tables or source edits are needed. Export each result to a separate
Excel sheet. Both queries use complete account populations for selected dates.

1. RDS_ratio_small_denominator_check.sql
Four fields: loan/income, repayment/income, savings/repayment, recent/prior balance.
Includes spike and neighbouring months identified in the MoM screening. These
selected windows are diagnostic coverage, not every date in the source history.
Up to 99 rows: 33 field/month combinations times three denominator cutoffs.
No rows for a requested month means absent source data, not a passed test.

Columns:
FIELD_NAME / POU_DT: field and reporting month.
DENOM_CUTOFF: cumulative small-positive-denominator scenario (1, 10, 100).
  These are exploratory amount cutoffs, not approved materiality thresholds.
  Repayment/income uses MONTHLY income; loan/income uses ANNUAL income.
REVIEW_STATUS: require RECONCILED before attributing results to the stored inputs.
VALID_COUNT: all non-NULL, non-special stored ratios (agreement-level weighting).
RECONCILED_COUNT: valid ratios matching the quotient of valid stored inputs.
  Matching tolerance: max(0.000001, abs(stored ratio)*0.000001), solely for
  arithmetic reconciliation, not an account-jump or business tolerance.
MEAN_ALL: mean of valid stored ratios; reconcile to the profiling run first.
SMALL_DENOM_COUNT / SHARE: valid ratios with valid inputs and 0 < denominator
  <= cutoff. SHARE uses VALID_COUNT as its denominator.
ABS_RATIO_CONTRIB_SHARE: share of the sum of absolute ratio values from that group.
  A magnitude-concentration diagnostic, not share of errors or model impact.
  Absolute values avoid cancellation from negative ratios. Compare MEAN_ALL and
  MEAN_WITHOUT_SMALL to assess the signed mean, not this share alone.
MEAN_WITHOUT_SMALL: diagnostic mean after excluding that group; it is not a
  recommendation to remove observations or a corrected model input.
MIN_POS_DENOM: lowest positive valid denominator among valid ratios.
All shares are fractions (0.95 = 95%); NULL means not calculable/suppressed.

Interpretation:
If a small fraction of accounts has small denominators, contributes most ratio
magnitude, and excluding it substantially removes the mean spike relative to
neighbouring months, this supports the Issue 5 mathematical mechanism.
If the reduced mean still spikes, or reconciliation fails, do not conclude the
same explanation. Investigate numerator movements, different input versions,
formula differences, population composition or other causes.
Matching arithmetic cannot prove underlying tiny income/repayment/balance values
are correct, intentional or acceptable for modelling. Development must assess that.
A generic input missingness limitation does not explain extreme positive ratios.

2. RDS_delinquency_tail_check.sql
Tests July 2018 and August 2020 plus adjacent months. Seven output columns:
POU_DT; REVIEW_STATUS; VALID_COUNT; MEAN_ALL; TOP_1PCT_COUNT;
TOP_1PCT_ABS_CONTRIB; MEAN_WITHOUT_TOP_1PCT.
The top 1% (rounded up) is ranked by absolute ratio, ties broken by AGMT_ID.
This can show concentration in the tail, but CANNOT establish small denominators.
CUST_ALL_MT_MAX_DELINQ_BASE_RT is a maximum over related mortgage/term accounts.
The main agreement's repayment is not necessarily the originating denominator.
For direct proof we need the source relationship/join logic, the related-account
DELINQUENCY_BASE_RT records and their repayment/arrears/balance inputs, matched
by reporting month, and the applicable closure/split/maturity conditions. Tied
maxima must be retained. Do not infer the winning related account from this output.

Separate matters:
CHQ missingness in 2021/22 and the PCKG_REPAY_AMT mean decline in Sep 2020 are
not ratio denominators and cannot directly be proved to be Issue 5 by these tests.

Controls and verification:
Confirmed common special list is embedded explicitly, including 9999. Duplicate
or missing agreement keys suppress output statistics. Source dates are month-end;
source must match the original profiling version. Compare means/counts to MoM.
Query arithmetic and controls were tested using SQLite-adapted synthetic cases;
queries have not been executed against live Teradata.
